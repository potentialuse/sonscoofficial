Terms & Conditions

Clear & Fair Policies

Our terms are designed for transparency and mutual trust. A formal agreement is signed before any project begins.

1
Pricing Policy
All rates based on covered area (sq.ft). Final cost may vary by design, soil, and material selection. Market fluctuations apply.
2
Drawing & Design
Client must provide approved drawings. Changes after work begins are charged separately. 3D designs quoted separately.
3
Payment Schedule
10% advance → 20% on foundation → 20% on structure completion → remaining in finishing stages.
4
Material & Quality
Material as per agreed package (Grey/Furnished/Luxury). Any upgrade increases cost accordingly.
5
Project Timeline
Duration depends on size & design. Delays may occur due to weather, material shortage, or client-side factors.
6
Exclusions
Government fees, utility connections, and boundary walls are not included unless specifically agreed in the contract.
7
Warranty
3–5 year structural warranty (where applicable). Finishing warranty depends on material brand specifications.
8
Site Responsibility
Client provides clear site possession plus water & electricity access. Extra charges apply if unavailable.
